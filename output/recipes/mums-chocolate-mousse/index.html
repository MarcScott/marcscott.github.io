Mum's Chocolate Mousse

« All recipes

Ingredients

  • 6oz plain chocolate
  • 2-3 tablespoons water, or black coffee
  • ½oz butter
  • 3 eggs

Method

  1. Break the chocolate into small pieces, put into a pan with the liquid and stir to a thick cream. The chocolate should be hot, but the sides of the pan never so hot that you cannot touch them.
  2. Take off the heat and stir in the butter and flavouring.
  3. Crack each of the eggs, putting the whites into a basin and dropping the yolks one at a time into the chocolate pan, stirring well after each addition. Watch point: it's important that the chocolate is hot so that the eggs get slightly cooked — be careful not to turn the eggs into scrambled eggs.
  4. Whisk the whites to a firm snow, then stir briskly into the chocolate — briskly, not a beating.
  5. Put in pots or a bowl.
