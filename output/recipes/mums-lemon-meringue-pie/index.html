Mum's Lemon Meringue Pie

« All recipes

Ingredients

  • 8oz shortcrust pastry — I make it with 8oz flour & 5oz marg
  • ½pt water — might need less if you prefer a stiffer filling
  • 3 large lemons
  • 2oz caster sugar, for the filling
  • 2oz cornflour — more again if you want a thicker filling
  • 2 eggs
  • 6oz caster sugar, for the meringue

Method

  1. Line a pie dish with the pastry and bake blind at 230°C (gas mark 8) for about 25 minutes. Use greaseproof paper and dried beans to keep the paper in place.
  2. Boil together the water, lemon juice, grated lemon rind and the 2oz sugar. Add the blended cornflour — make sure you blend it well as it will go lumpy if you don't.
  3. Boil for 3 minutes, stirring well, then remove the pan from the heat and beat in the egg yolks. Just make sure the filling is not too runny or thick. Pour into the cooked pastry shell.
  4. Whisk the egg whites — add 2 or 3 more whites to make a very big topping — then whisk in the 6oz caster sugar.
  5. Spread the meringue over the filling and dredge with sugar.
  6. Bake the pie and top in a very slow oven, 135°C (gas mark ½), for about 1 hour.
