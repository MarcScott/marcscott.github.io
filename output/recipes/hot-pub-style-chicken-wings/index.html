Hot Pub-Style Chicken Wings

« All recipes

Ingredients

For the wings

  • 2 kg chicken wings
  • 2 tbsp baking powder
  • 2 tbsp paprika
  • 1 tbsp cayenne
  • ½ tbsp garlic salt

For the sauce

  • 60 g butter
  • 6 tbsp Frank's hot sauce
  • 1 tbsp sugar

Method

  1. Prep the wings — cut into drums and flats, then pat very dry with kitchen paper.
  2. Season — in a large bowl, toss the wings with the baking powder, paprika, cayenne and garlic salt until evenly coated.
  3. Bake — arrange the wings on a wire rack over a baking tray and bake at 120°C for 30 minutes.
  4. Make the sauce — while the wings are cooking, melt the butter in a saucepan over low heat. Stir in the Frank's hot sauce and sugar until smooth.
  5. Sauce the wings — toss the hot wings in the sauce until fully coated.
  6. Crisp — increase the oven to 220°C and cook for another 45 minutes, turning once halfway through, until crisp and cooked through.
  7. Optional finish — toss with your choice of Hot Ones Last Dab sauce.
