## Ingredients
- 750g beef mince
- 2 large onions
- 2 large bell peppers
- 2 cloves of garlic
- 1 jar of black olives
- 2 red wine stock pots
- 3 tins of tomatoes
- 1/2 a tube of tomato paste
- 1 fistful of fresh basil
- salt to taste
- pepper to taste
- 1 tbsp oregano
- 1 tbsp sage
- 1 tsp thyme

## Method
1. Fry onions.
1. Add mince and brown.
1. Chop and add peppers and fry for a bit longer.
1. Crush garlic and add with rest of the ingredients.
1. Simmer for an hour.
