## Ingredients
- 1.25kg split pea lentils
- 1.3L oil
- 10 Medium sized potatoes (about 1.5kg)
- Citric acid
- Sugar
- Turmeric
- Chilli powder
- 500g Cashews
- 600g Almonds
- 700g Peanuts
- 300g Sultanas
- Bag of rice flakes?
- Powdered food colouring
- 10 Dried red chillies
- Heaped tbsp Coriander seed
- Tbsp Mustard seeds
- 2 Tbsp sesame seeds
- 20 Curry leaves
## Method
- Soak lentils for 24 hours
- Add oil to a large wok and heat.
- Grate potatoes and wash
- Strain lentils and add to oil. About a fist full at a time, until they float to surface.
- Add lentils to large pot, and 1 pinch citric acid, 3 pinches sugar, 1 pinch turmeric, 1/2 teaspoon chilli powder
- Cover the first layer in kitchen towel.
- Fry the cashews in two batches, until light brown, then add to pot.
- Add 1 pinch citric acid, 3 pinches sugar, 1 pinch turmeric, 1/2 teaspoon chilli powder and cover with kitchen towel
- Fry the almonds in two batches, until light brown, then add to pot.
- Add 1 pinch citric acid, 3 pinches sugar, 1 pinch turmeric, 1/2 teaspoon chilli powder and cover with kitchen towel
- Fry the peanuts in three batches, until light brown, then add to pot.
- Add 1 pinch citric acid, 3 pinches sugar, 1 pinch turmeric, 1/2 teaspoon chilli powder and cover with kitchen towel
- Fry sultanas until they all float and become puffy.
- Add 1 pinch citric acid, 3 pinches sugar, 1 pinch turmeric, 1/2 teaspoon chilli powder and cover with kitchen towel
- Fry rice flakes, a fistful at a time. Add six fistfuls in total.
- Colour the potatoes three different colours. Fry a fistful at a time after squeezing out the water. When crispy add to the pot.
- In a separate pan, fry the chillies, then add the coriander seed, mustard seed, sesame seed and curry leaves. It should all be heated until it darkens and smokes.
- Remove the paper towels from the pot, and make a big well in the centre.
- Add the fried spices, and then mix really well. Add more turmeric and chilli powder to taste.
