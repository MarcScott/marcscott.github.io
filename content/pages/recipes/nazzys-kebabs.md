## Ingredients
- 750 g beef mince
- 500 g lamb mince
- 8 green finger chillies
- 1 tbsp ground coriander
- 1 tbsp pepper
- 1 tbsp salt
- 2 tbsp gram flour
- 2 tbsp Marc's garam masala
- 1 tbsp smoked paprika
- 5 cloves of garlic
- 1 thumb of ginger

## Method
1. Mix the beef and lamb.
1. Mix all the dry ingredients, apart from the salt.
1. Crush the salt, garlic, chillies and ginger in a pestle and mortar.
1. Add both the dry mix and the crushed paste to the meat.
1. Shape into burgers.
1. Shallow fry.
