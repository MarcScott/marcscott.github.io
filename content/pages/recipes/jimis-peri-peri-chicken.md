## Ingredients
- 6 cloves garlic
- 4 red Fresno chili peppers, seeded
- 1 habanero pepper, seeded
- 1/2 cup diced red bell pepper, or other sweet pepper
- 1 tablespoon smoked paprika
- 1 tablespoon fresh thyme leaves
- 1 1/2 teaspoons kosher salt
- 1 teaspoon freshly ground black pepper
- 1 teaspoon ground cayenne pepper
- 1 teaspoon white sugar
- 1/2 teaspoon onion powder
- 1/2 cup mild olive oil, or vegetable oil
- 1/3 cup sherry vinegar
- 2 lemons, juiced
- 4 chicken leg/thigh quarters
- kosher salt to taste, to season the chicken

## Method
1. Blend everything except the chicken and the seasoning salt to a smooth
   marinade.
1. Season the chicken quarters all over with salt, then coat thoroughly in the
   marinade.
1. Cover and leave to marinate in the fridge for 24 hours.
1. Bake at 180°C for 45 minutes, or longer for melt-off-the-bone chicken.
