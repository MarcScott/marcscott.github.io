## Ingredients
### For the wings
- 2 kg chicken wings
- 2 tbsp baking powder
- 2 tbsp paprika
- 1 tbsp cayenne
- ½ tbsp garlic salt

### For the sauce
- 60 g butter
- 6 tbsp Frank's hot sauce
- 1 tbsp sugar

## Method
1. **Prep the wings** — cut into drums and flats, then pat very dry with
   kitchen paper.
1. **Season** — in a large bowl, toss the wings with the baking powder,
   paprika, cayenne and garlic salt until evenly coated.
1. **Bake** — arrange the wings on a wire rack over a baking tray and bake at
   120°C for 30 minutes.
1. **Make the sauce** — while the wings are cooking, melt the butter in a
   saucepan over low heat. Stir in the Frank's hot sauce and sugar until
   smooth.
1. **Sauce the wings** — toss the hot wings in the sauce until fully coated.
1. **Crisp** — increase the oven to 220°C and cook for another 45 minutes,
   turning once halfway through, until crisp and cooked through.
1. **Optional finish** — toss with your choice of Hot Ones Last Dab sauce.
