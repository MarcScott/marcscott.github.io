## Ingredients
- 1kg beef or mutton
- 6 cups of water
- 2 Heaped teaspoon of salt
- 20 pepper corns
- 1 cinamon stick
- 8 bay leaves
- 6 cardamon pods
- 1 tbsp cumin seeds
- 20 whole cloves
- 2 cloves of garlic
- 2 thumb sized sticks of ginger
- 2 star anise
- 1 large onion
- 2 large potatoes
- 3 cups of rice
## Method
- Into a preassure cooker add the cubed meat and 6 cups of water
- Halve all the ingredients listed above (after the water), with the exception of the onion, potatoes and rice.
- Add all of it to the pressure cooker. Crush the garlic and thin slice the ginger before adding.
- Add six cups of water.
- Pressure cook for 20 minutes
- Add all the spices, salt etc that are left (so half of the above) to a pan with a little oil. Fry them all for a couple of minutes.
- Thin slice the onion and add that. Fry until **very** brown and almost burning.
- Add the ginger and garlic and fry for a little while longer.
- Add the fried onions and spices to a larger caserole dish, along with the pressure cooked meat, water and spices.
- Place in a preheated oven 1t 180°C for about 50 minutes, or until rice is the right texture. Add more water or cook for longer if neccessary.
