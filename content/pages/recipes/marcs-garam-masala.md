Title: Marc's Garam Masala
Slug: recipes/marcs-garam-masala
Group: Spice Blends
Template: recipe

Makes approximately 125g.

## Ingredients
- 35g coriander seeds
- 30g cumin seeds
- 17g green cardamom pods
- 12g cloves
- 9–10g black peppercorns
- 8g Ceylon cinnamon, or 4g if using cassia cinnamon

### Optional but recommended
- 4 dried Indian bay leaves (about 1g)
- 3 star anise (about 4g)
- 8g fennel seeds
- ½ small nutmeg (about 2g)
- 3–4 black cardamom pods (3–4g)
- 3 strands mace

## Method
1. Check the spices for grit or debris. Wipe the cinnamon and bay leaves clean
   and break open the nutmeg to check it.
1. Heat a dry frying pan over a low heat.
1. Toast the cardamoms, bay leaves, star anise, nutmeg, mace, cinnamon, cloves
   and peppercorns until aromatic and crisp. Transfer to a plate.
1. Toast the coriander seeds until lightly browned and aromatic. Transfer to
   the plate.
1. Briefly toast the fennel seeds until aromatic, then transfer to the plate.
1. Toast the cumin seeds until aromatic and slightly darker. Do not let any of
   the spices burn.
1. Allow everything to cool completely.
1. Grind to a fine powder in 40–60 second bursts. Sieve and regrind any coarse
   pieces if necessary.
1. Store in a clean, dry, airtight glass jar.
